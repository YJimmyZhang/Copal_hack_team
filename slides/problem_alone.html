THE PROBLEM TODAY

Everyone stands alone in the deal

Business succession is fragmented: each party acts on its own, too late and without the full picture.
The owner Searches alone, often too late

Starts only when age or health forces it. Relies on brokers, listings and personal network, with no clear view of the company's value or exit options.

The successor Ready to lead, but invisible

Has the skills and some capital, but no way to reach owners who are quietly preparing to sell. Financing stays uncertain until late in the deal.

KBC Sees the signals, stays passive

Already holds the data that predicts a succession: owner age, shareholding, investment, cash. Yet joins only at the end, as one of several lenders.
